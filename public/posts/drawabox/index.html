My draw a box experience: introduction
Documenting the first day of my draw a box experience
2026-09-12 16:09 folder Drawing   label reviews  

I have never been an artist, and although I enjoy drawing I never took it seriously I just draw for fun and was always okay with the fact that I am bad at it, I mostly drew doodles or landscapes for my journal and when I was younger I drew on A4 papers as an activity with my sisters and didn’t really care much for the end results

So drawing has always been about the activity itself rather than a mean of self expression or a hobby that I took seriously I also don’t have the talent for it at all and my drawings (like my handwriting) looks like a five years old'

The reason I am deciding to start draw a box is because I want my journals to look more like me and reflect the feelings I want to to capture and lately writing seems like isn’t enough for this mission. I also constantly have this feeling like I am overflowing with feelings, thoughts and opinions with little to no means of expression so along with writing I want to be able to express myself in little drawing as well

I don’t aim for realism by starting this course, I only want to be able to sketch landscapes or simple drawings of media I am consming, places I am describing and simply making my journal pages more expressive

As you may have noticed by reading my blog, I am not the best at writing either, english is my third language and while I am very good at writing in my other two languages I attempt documenting in this blog in english because I want to also get better at this

Why draw a box?

The simple answer is that I like the structure of it, how it is shaped like a ciriculm and starts by the basics and you can by the end of each session see the progress

I also like that it is dense in terms of reading because along with getting better at drawing I am also keeping my attention span intact this summer before university starts again I will probably go back to this section and update this as I get to appreciate the course more but for now this is my main motivation: get better at drawing starting from the basics

Lesson one review

The picture featured in this blog article is one page from my homework of lesson one Superimposed Lines

It is about drawing lines over eachother with as little variance as possible, I saw the imporvement immediatly after switching from wrist to shoulder as the lesson require, and as I drew more lines the improvement became more and more visible, the longer the line the harder it was, and the half circles were a challenge but by the second page the variance grew smaller and the lines became more steady and it became more automatic for me to use my shoulder which honestly was easier to adapt to than I expected

This blog article is a first draft that I will come back to and update the writing but not the content because I do believe that I want my blog to be edited and polished but I also want to capture the reviews as they happen to see not only the progress of my drawing skills but also my writing and documentation skills which are equally important for my goal which is to have a more capturing journal

Advices for people starting draw a box

I think by this stage the only advice I have is to focus on improving by each line you draw rather than falling back into auto mode of drawing line over line if you do this, you will see the visible progress by the end of the homeworks

Thanks for reading
