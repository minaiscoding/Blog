Reflections on Hollow Knight
I played this game twice, once when I was very stressed and another when I was calm here is my review
2026-08-27 13:52 folder Games   label reflection   review  

Context

I will assume by reading my blog you already know and played hollow knight and I will be spooiling it so skip this one if you haven’t finish it yet and come back to reflect with me once you’re done playing

I played Hollow knight twice in my life but I only remember one playthrough the is because during the first one I was heavily disassociating and very stressed and I used the game simply as a distraction and to kill time as I tend to do when I am in a tight period of time where I just try to skip it

now I am playing the game relaxed and happy in my life and it’s a totally different experience and it made me reflect how this is also true in real life, stress puts a whole cloud on your existence to the point where you don’t enjoy nor notice anything in your life you simply stop experiencing the whole to avoid experiencing the scary
